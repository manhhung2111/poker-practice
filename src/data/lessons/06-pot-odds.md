## Two numbers decide every call

When someone bets into you after the flop, compare two numbers:

- **The price**: how much [[equity|equity]] you need to call. This is the [[pot-odds|pot odds]].
- **Your equity**: how often your hand wins against the hands they likely have.

> If your equity is bigger than the price, call. If it's smaller, fold.

## Working out the price

The equity you need = your call ÷ the pot after you call.

Pot is 10bb and they bet 5bb (half the pot). You call 5bb into a final pot of 20bb: 5 ÷ 20 = **25%**.

You don't need to do this at the table every time. Remember the common sizes:

| They bet | You need |
|---|---|
| ⅓ pot | 20% |
| ½ pot | 25% |
| ⅔ pot | 29% |
| Pot | 33% |

Bigger bets need more equity to call. Small bets can be called with a lot of hands.

## Your equity: think in ranges

You never know their exact cards. Think about the [[range|range]] of hands they would bet like this. A [[nit|nit]] betting big has a strong range; a [[maniac|maniac]] bets with almost anything. The same hand can be a call against one and a fold against the other.

Turn on **Show equity** in the table settings to see the app's estimate while you play.

## Draws: count your outs

With a [[draw|draw]], count your [[outs|outs]], the cards that make your hand. Then use the rule of 2 and 4:

- On the flop (two cards to come): outs × 4 ≈ your equity.
- On the turn (one card to come): outs × 2.

| Draw | Outs | Flop | Turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flush draw | 9 | ~35% | ~19% |
| Open-ended straight draw | 8 | ~32% | ~17% |
| Gutshot | 4 | ~17% | ~9% |

A flush draw on the flop facing a half-pot bet (25% needed) is a clear call. The same draw on the turn facing a pot-sized bet (33% needed) is a fold, unless you count the extra chips you win when you hit: [[implied-odds|implied odds]]. The app credits draws with about 5% for this.

## Raise when you're far ahead

With a very strong hand (about 70% equity or more), raise for [[value|value]]: worse hands will still call. With a medium hand that just beats the price, calling is enough.

## Key points

- Call when your equity beats the price; fold when it doesn't.
- Price: ½ pot needs 25%, pot needs 33%.
- Draws: outs × 4 on the flop, × 2 on the turn.
- Strong hands raise; medium hands call.
