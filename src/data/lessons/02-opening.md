## Raise or fold, never limp

When everyone folds to you, you have two choices: **raise to 2.5bb** (3bb from the SB) or fold. That first raise is called an [[open|open]], or RFI (raise first in).

Don't just call the big blind ([[limp|limping]]). A raise can win the blinds right away, builds a pot with your good hands, and gives you the [[initiative|initiative]] after the flop.

## How wide each seat opens

These are the opening [[range|ranges]] in this app, as a share of all starting hands:

| Seat | Opens | Rough idea |
|---|---|---|
| UTG | 11% | 77+, AT+ suited, AJ+ offsuit, KQ |
| UTG+1 | 12% | adds 66, A9s, T9s |
| MP | 15% | adds 55, more suited aces, ATo, KJo |
| LJ | 19% | all suited aces, QJo |
| HJ | 24% | 33+, more suited kings and connectors |
| CO | 28% | all pairs, many suited hands, A8o+ |
| BTN | 46% | almost any suited hand, most broadways |
| SB | 38% | wide, but you'll be out of position vs the BB |

Notice that each seat's range includes everything the seat before it opens, plus more.

## What gets added as ranges widen

Roughly in this order:

- **Pairs.** Even 22 can win a big pot when it hits a [[set|set]].
- **Suited aces.** They make the [[nuts|nut flush]], and A5s–A2s can make a [[wheel|wheel]].
- **Strong [[broadway|broadway]] hands** like KQ, KJ, QJ.
- **[[suited-connector|Suited connectors]]** like T9s, 87s, 65s.
- **Offsuit hands last.** Offsuit hands can't make flushes and are often [[dominated|dominated]].

## Suited vs offsuit

Being [[suited|suited]] only adds a few percent of [[equity|equity]], but those flushes win big pots. That's why K6s is an open from the CO while K6o is a fold: the suited version has much better [[playability|playability]].

## Key points

- Raise or fold. Open 2.5bb (3bb from the SB).
- Ranges grow from 11% at UTG to 46% on the button.
- Add pairs, suited aces, broadways and suited connectors before offsuit junk.
- Learn the UTG and BTN ranges first, then fill in the seats between.
